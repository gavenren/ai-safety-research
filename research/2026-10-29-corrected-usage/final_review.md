## Proofs and statistical interpretation

The finite-prefix proof is complete. Substituting the induction hypothesis into `qk=max(0,q(k-1)+dk)` gives `qk=Sk-min(Sk,m(k-1))`. The empty prefix establishes the base case and ensures the minimum is nonpositive. At complete receipt, the signed changes telescope to the final charge. Thus the final shared clipping error is the negative of the smallest prefix sum.

Applying the same argument to each item gives the item-clipping result. Each item sum at a shared prefix is at least its own minimum. Therefore the minimum shared sum is at least the sum of the separate minima. This proves the stated ItemClamp ordering. The separate minima need not occur at the same time. The MaxReplace expression and exactness arguments for Net and RevisionReplace are also correct under the stated identity, delta, revision, and closure assumptions.

The guard argument is correctly conditional. If q equals the final charge, it makes exactly the feasible decision. If q is larger, it can refuse feasible work but cannot admit an excess-charge branch. This does not authorize an incomplete signed balance as available budget, nor does it prove a physical-use limit.

No population failure rate, confidence interval, or significance claim is made. The selected ranges, dependent branches, overlapping summary groups, and duplicate-group weighting are disclosed. In particular, different frequencies between duplicate multiset groups do not isolate the causal effect of exact replay.
