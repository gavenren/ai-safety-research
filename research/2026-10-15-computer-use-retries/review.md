# Research checklist review

Date: 2026-10-15. Scope: the 48-trial additional website extension and the combined paper. Relevant parts of the [NeurIPS Paper Checklist](https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist) were used as a review aid. This is not a conference submission, certification, or approval claim.

- Claims: the abstract and Section 10 match saved counts. The study is a selected replication on public demonstrations. It does not claim a new retry rule or production reliability.
- Assumptions and limits: search scope, visible evidence restrictions, default rows, temporary state, no service audit, unavailable Receipt, shared contexts, and site selection are explicit. Earlier experiments remain separate.
- Method: question, hypotheses, controls, measures, deadline, and fixed assignments were saved before scored writes. The local freeze is not external preregistration. The early failed feasibility read remains available.
- Reproduction: the main analyzer and separate replay reproduce their saved outputs in separate copies. Raw task observations, action arguments, times, all assignments, images, hashes, versions, and commands are included. Live trajectories can differ.
- Uncertainty: four selected repeats per cell are descriptive. No population intervals or significance claim is made. Model and tool time is not website latency.
- Failures: setup errors, D03's clipped original image, the later corrected image, and the failed early timing gate remain documented. No trial was replaced. No scored action error or rule violation was found.
- Resources: existing Codex service and browser only. No training, purchased API service, or new paid compute. Exact model build and inference hardware are unavailable.
- Rights and privacy: synthetic inputs and attributed task UI only. The public extracts omit unrelated navigation and application-code samples. All 145 public images were checked. A full private fallback image is excluded with its hash retained. No new license is assigned. Font notices were updated for the six actual embedded subsets; license text is unchanged.
- Ethics: no messages, purchases, private account records, harmful instructions, or permission changes were used in this extension. All temporary table additions were cleared by reload after the primary audit.
- Review status: Codex generated the work. Separate agents reviewed the method, results, original/public evidence, and PDF. There was no human or external peer review.
