# Data attribution and license

The source data comes from the **AI Incident Database**, maintained by the **Responsible AI Collaborative and its contributors**. CSET supplied the CSET classification fields.

Source: [AIID snapshot archive](https://incidentdatabase.ai/research/snapshots/).

The AIID [terms of use](https://incidentdatabase.ai/terms-of-use/) license the incident and classification collections under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The `data_*.csv` extracts retain that license. The adapted data in `results_*.csv`, `results_summary.json`, and the data figures also carry CC BY-SA 4.0. This follows the source license. It is not a new license for the repository as a whole.

Changes: selected factual fields; removed descriptions, people and entity names, and article text; sorted by incident ID; grouped dates; joined classifications by ID; calculated counts, coverage, and bounds; drew figures. No source records were silently replaced with synthetic data.

The original archive notice states that report contents have their own intellectual property rights. The report text from third-party articles is not redistributed here.

No endorsement by AIID, CSET, Stanford, or any other source organization is claimed. Source data is supplied as is. See the full license for its terms and notices.
